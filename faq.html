Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about laptops, orders and your account.

Choosing a laptop

Which laptop should I get?

Start with what you'll do most. For school and travel, look at the Ultrabooks or the Campus 14. For games, the Gaming laptops. For photo and video work, Creator / Pro. You can also compare up to three side by side.

Are the battery numbers real?

The “up to” numbers come from the manufacturer. The “Our take” section on each product page says how long it lasted in our own month of use, which is usually a bit less.

Do you sell refurbished laptops?

No. Every laptop we sell is new and sealed.

Orders and shipping

How fast is shipping?

Standard shipping is free and takes 2 business days. Express is next business day for $19. See Shipping & returns.

Can I return a laptop?

Yes, within 30 days of delivery for any reason, with a free return label.

Where can I see my orders?

Log in and open your account. Every order you place is listed there.

Your account

Why do I need an account to check out?

Your account keeps your cart and order history together, so they're still there next time you log in.

My account disappeared. What happened?

Accounts are saved in your browser (see Privacy). If you cleared your browser data or switched devices, you'll need to sign up again.

Need help?

We answer within one business day.
