Shipping & returns

Free 2‑day shipping on every laptop, and 30 days to change your mind.

Shipping

OptionDelivery timeCost
Standard2 business daysFree
ExpressNext business day$19.00

Orders placed before 2pm on a business day ship the same day. We ship to all 50 US states and Washington, D.C. Every laptop ships in a padded box and needs a signature on delivery.

Returns

You have 30 days from delivery to return any laptop for a full refund, for any reason.

  1. Contact us with your order number and pick “Returns and warranty”.
  2. We email you a free, prepaid return label.
  3. Pack the laptop with its charger and drop it off at any UPS location.
  4. We refund you within 5 business days of receiving it.

Please back up your files and sign out of your accounts first. We wipe every returned laptop.

Damaged on arrival?

Tell us within 7 days and we'll send a replacement right away. You don't need to wait for the return.

Need help?

We answer within one business day.
